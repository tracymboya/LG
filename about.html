About Our School

Our history, mission, vision and values

Our History

St Mary's Lwak Girls High School is a girls' secondary school in the Lwak area of Siaya County, Kenya. This page introduces our school and its learning environment.

Our Mission

To provide quality education that nurtures talent, builds character and prepares learners for the future.

Our Vision

To encourage academic achievement, discipline and personal development.

Our Values

  • Discipline and responsibility
  • Respect and integrity
  • Hard work and teamwork
  • Academic excellence
